PRIMARY ACCENTS / V2 / FLUENT 2 BRAND RAMPS

Primary palettes

Each of the 17 primary accents as a Fluent 2 (web) BrandVariants ramp: 16 steps, 10 darkest to 160 lightest, with the accent at 80. The ramps are tuned so Fluent’s default light and dark token mappings pass WCAG AA without overrides.

Light: brand background 80 · hover 70 · selected 60 · pressed 40 · tint 160 · brand text and links 80, ≥4.5:1 on #FFFFFF, #FAFAFA, #F5F5F5, #EBEBEB and the ramp’s own 160. Dark: brand text and links 100, ≥4.5:1 on #141414, #1F1F1F, #292929, #383838 and #3D3D3D.

11 accents are darkened slightly at 80 to clear AA as brand text; the original hex is noted under each. 80 · key is where the accent sits. Dark text marks the steps where #242B3B text passes AA (100–160). White text passes AA on 10–80; 90 passes neither for body text.
