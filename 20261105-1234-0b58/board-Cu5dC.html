What works well instead

Categorical

One shared categorical palette of 8–10 colours for multi-series charts, always in the same order.

Status

One shared semantic palette for status (on track, at risk, failed).

Chart accent

One “chart accent” colour that changes per product. Use it only for single-series charts, highlighted bars and KPI sparklines.
