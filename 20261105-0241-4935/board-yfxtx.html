What could go wrong here?

Claude

The main risk is old safety data. If a doctor adds an allergy, another doctor could see the old record for up to 5 minutes. Names and addresses change rarely, so they are fine to cache.

Can we cache only the safe parts?

Claude

Yes. Split the record: cache name, age and contacts for 5 minutes, and always fetch allergies and medications from the EHR. The patient screen still opens in about 5 ms. The allergy list takes about 400 ms.
