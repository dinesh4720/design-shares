The story

TWO ORPHANS.
ONE THRONE.

Asta and Yuno were left on the steps of the same church on the same night. Yuno grew up a prodigy with the wind at his call. Asta grew up shouting, training, and failing to cast so much as a spark.

At fifteen, Yuno was chosen by a legendary four‑leaf grimoire. Asta got nothing, until a tattered black five‑leaf book found him, and with it a sword that cancels magic itself.

Both swore to become Wizard King. Only one of them can.
