04 / Process

Four steps.
No black boxes.

How an engagement runs

You'll always know what we're doing, what it costs and what you get at the end. The same four steps, whether it's a website or a forty-screen product.

01

Listen

Two weeks inside your product, your analytics and your support tickets. We watch five real customers use what you've built.

Weeks 1–2

02

Map

We find the patterns worth keeping, name them, and agree the rules in a working session with your team.

Weeks 2–3

03

Design & build

We design in components, not screens, and ship the front-end with them. You see a working build every Friday.

Weeks 3–9

04

Hand over

Documentation, tokens and a recorded walkthrough, so your team can extend the system without us.

Week 10

Every Friday: a working build, a changelog and a 30-minute call.
